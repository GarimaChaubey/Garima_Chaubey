Garima Chaubey

Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science & Engineering, researching how AI systems learn, adapt, and remember across a lifetime overcoming #Catastrophic-Forgetting.

I am a Ph.D. researcher at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad, where my work sits at the intersection of continual learning, representation learning, and multi-agent reinforcement environments.

My research explores how artificial agents can retain past knowledge while flexibly adapting to new tasks — a question motivated by both cognitive science and the practical demands of lifelong AI. I design hybrid frameworks combining Reinforced Continual Learning, Bayesian optimization, and CLIP-based representation learning to balance stability and plasticity in evolving environments.

Before my doctoral studies, I completed an M.Sc. in Computational Science and Applications from Banaras Hindu University, where my thesis developed a Bayesian Logistic Regression framework for modelling well-being outcomes among university staff.

Beyond research, I enjoy reading about statistical theory, exploring ideas in neuroscience, and mentoring juniors interested in applied machine learning.
